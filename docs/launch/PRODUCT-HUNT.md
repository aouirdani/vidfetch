# Product Hunt draft — VidFetch

**Tagline**

Save public TikTok videos, or convert your own video files in your browser

**Description**

VidFetch brings together two separate workflows:

- Paste a public TikTok video link to prepare an available MP4 or an MP3 soundtrack. No VidFetch account is required. Availability depends on the post, the platform and service limits; a public post is not automatically yours to reuse.
- Choose a video file on your device to extract MP3/M4A, compress, trim or make a GIF. These local tools run in your browser. The selected file is not uploaded to VidFetch for conversion.

The local tools include explicit file and duration limits, an output-size check, progress, cancellation and browser storage handling. Codec support varies by device. For TikTok, VidFetch saves the version supplied by the source and does not remove an embedded watermark. YouTube is unavailable. Instagram and X pages are not being advertised as supported services.

Use only files you own or have permission to process, and only save platform content where you have the right to do so. Terms, privacy and open-source notices are linked on the site.

**Topics**

Video, audio, browser tools, privacy

**Maker comment**

Hi Product Hunt — VidFetch started as a small TikTok link utility. We added a different set of browser-local tools for people who need to trim, compress, extract audio from, or make a GIF from a file already on their device. Those conversions happen locally; the platform-link workflow is separate and has its own service limits. I’d value feedback on the mobile flow, file-size guidance and compatibility messages.
