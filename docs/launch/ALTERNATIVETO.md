# AlternativeTo listing draft

**Name**: VidFetch

**Short description**: Browser-local video tools for your own files, plus a separate MP4/MP3 workflow for available public TikTok posts.

**Description**: VidFetch includes local browser tools to extract MP3 or compatible AAC as M4A, compress, trim and create short GIFs from files selected on your device. Those conversions do not upload the selected file to VidFetch. Limits and format support depend on the tool and browser. A separate service can prepare available MP4/MP3 renditions from public TikTok links, subject to platform and service restrictions. It does not remove an embedded watermark. YouTube is unavailable; Instagram and X are not listed as verified services.

**Platforms**: Web

**License**: Free to use; source code for bundled local components and notices are linked on the site. (Do not select “open source” for the whole product unless its repository license is verified.)

**Alternatives/tags**: video converter, video compressor, video trimmer, video to GIF, TikTok video utility.

**Submission checklist**: confirm live URLs, current limits and production behavior; do not assert multi-platform support, unlimited usage, no watermark, 4K guarantees, ratings or user counts.
