## Procedure for the publisher’s own test

Open TikTok’s current official instructions and perform the account-data request on your own account. Record the app version, language, device, observed menu names, available choices and request date. When an export is supplied, record the actual receipt date, format and categories present without publishing personal data. Compare its contents with the requested categories. Distinguish account records from saved video files and identify any limitations stated in the current official notice.
